# Banking Project Appraisal & KYC Pre-Audit Standards (NABARD / SBI / PNB)

## 1. Mandatory KYC & Pre-Audit Verification
- **Aadhaar Card**: Demographic validation (Name, DOB matching bank passbook).
- **PAN Card**: Mandatory for loans above ₹50,000 and GST registration.
- **Udyam MSME Registration Certificate**: Instant zero-cost registration via Udyam portal.
- **Record of Rights (RoR) / Khatian / Rent Agreement**: Proof of place of business in West Bengal.
- **Bank Account Passbook / Statement**: Last 6 months bank transaction records.

## 2. Bank Appraisal Benchmarks (2025 Standard)
- **Minimum DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)**: 1.50x or above.
- **Maximum Break-Even Point (BEP)**: $\le 55\%$ rated capacity.
- **Current Ratio**: $\ge 1.33x$ for working capital assessment.
- **Promoter Equity Margin**: Minimum 10% for General and 5% for Special rural categories.

## 3. Strict Anti-Hallucination & Banking Verification Protocols
- Verbal collection of confidential Aadhaar numbers, PAN or OTPs is strictly forbidden.
- Always include: "Bank final decision lega."
- Every metric cited must reference RBI/NABARD/PMEGP appraisal norms.
