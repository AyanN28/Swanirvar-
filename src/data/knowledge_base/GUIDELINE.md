# PMEGP & PMFME Operational Guidelines (2025)

## 1. PMEGP Margin Money Subsidy Rates (Ministry of MSME)
- **Special Category (Rural)**:
  - Beneficiaries: SC / ST / OBC / Minorities / Women / Ex-servicemen / Differently-abled / NER / Hill and Border areas.
  - Own Contribution (Promoter Margin): 5% of project cost.
  - Government Margin Money Subsidy: 35% of project cost in Rural areas, 25% in Urban areas.
- **General Category (Rural)**:
  - Own Contribution: 10% of project cost.
  - Government Margin Money Subsidy: 25% of project cost in Rural areas, 15% in Urban areas.

## 2. Maximum Project Ceiling Under PMEGP (2025)
- Manufacturing Sector: Up to ₹50 Lakhs.
- Service / Business Sector: Up to ₹20 Lakhs.
- Second Loan for Upgrading Existing PMEGP Units: Up to ₹1.00 Crore (Manufacturing) and ₹25 Lakhs (Service), with 15% subsidy.

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro food processing Enterprises (PMFME)
- 35% Credit-linked capital subsidy up to ₹10 Lakh per unit.
- One District One Product (ODOP) focus for value chains (e.g., Tea in Alipurduar/Jalpaiguri, Pineapple in Siliguri, Mango in Malda).
