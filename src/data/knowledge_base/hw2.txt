# HW2 Financial Analysis Reference & Verification Matrix (West Bengal 2025)

1. Margin Capital to Project Outlay Multiplier:
   - Margin Capital: Promoter's available liquid equity (typically 10% for general rural enterprises, 5% for special categories).
   - Base Multiplier: 10x available margin.
   - Example: ₹14,000 margin yields ₹1,40,000 total project cost (Loan ₹1,26,000 @ 6.5% for 3 years with 3-month moratorium).
   - Example: ₹1,00,000 margin yields ₹10,00,000 total project cost (Loan ₹9,00,000 @ 8.0% for 7 years with 6-month moratorium).

2. Working Capital & Capital Expenditure (Capex) Allocation:
   - Capex (Plant, Machinery & Tools): ~65% of Total Project Cost.
   - Civil Works / Shed / Setup: ~15% of Total Project Cost.
   - Working Capital (Raw materials, initial stock, wages): ~15% of Total Project Cost.
   - Preliminary & Contingency: ~5% of Total Project Cost.

3. EMI Calculation Post-Moratorium Formula:
   - Monthly rate $r = \frac{\text{Annual Rate}}{12 \times 100}$
   - Post-Moratorium Months $n = \text{Total Months} - \text{Moratorium Months}$
   - $\text{EMI} = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$

4. West Bengal Regional Subsidies (2025):
   - PMEGP West Bengal (DIC / KVIC): 35% Special Rural Subsidy, 25% General Rural Subsidy.
   - Bangla Swanirbhar Karmasansthan Prakalpa (BSKP): Up to 30% state margin money assistance.
   - PM SVANidhi: 7% interest subsidy on timely digital repayment.
