# Swanirvar Saathi Deterministic Financial Engine Specification

## 1. Mathematical Formulas & Core Engine Parameters
- **Project Cost Determination**:
  $$\text{Project Cost} = \frac{\text{Available Margin Capital}}{0.10}$$
- **Loan Amount Calculation**:
  $$\text{Loan Amount} = \text{Project Cost} \times 0.90$$
  $$\text{Promoter Margin Capital} = \text{Project Cost} \times 0.10$$

## 2. Scheme Thresholds & Interest Matrix (2025)
1. **Tier 1: Micro Finance Scheme (Project Cost $\le$ ₹1.40 Lakh / ₹1,40,000)**:
   - Interest Rate: 6.5% per annum
   - Repayment Tenor: 3 Years (36 Months total)
   - Moratorium Period: 3 Months initial principal grace period
   - Repayment Months: 33 Post-Moratorium EMI installments
   - Standard Eligibility: PM SVANidhi, MUDRA Shishu/Kishore, State Rural Livelihood Mission micro-finance.

2. **Tier 2: Term Loan / PMEGP / Stand-Up India (Project Cost > ₹1.40 Lakh and $\le$ ₹50.00 Lakh)**:
   - Interest Rate: 8.0% per annum
   - Repayment Tenor: 7 Years (84 Months total)
   - Moratorium Period: 6 Months initial grace period
   - Repayment Months: 78 Post-Moratorium EMI installments
   - Standard Eligibility: PMEGP, PMFME, MUDRA Tarun, Stand-Up India.

## 3. Financial Metrics & Feasibility Ratios
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)**:
  $$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Operating Profit (EBIDTA)} + \text{Depreciation}}{\text{Annual Principal Repayment} + \text{Annual Interest On Term Loan}}$$
  - Acceptable RBI Benchmark: $\ge 1.50\text{x}$ (Preferred: $1.75\text{x} - 2.40\text{x}$)
- **Break-Even Point (BEP)**:
  $$\text{BEP (\%)} = \frac{\text{Total Fixed Costs}}{\text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Total Variable Costs}} \times 100$$
  - Target: $\le 55\%$ Capacity Utilization in Year 1.
- **3-Year Profit & Loss Projections**:
  - Year 1: Base output operating at 60% rated capacity.
  - Year 2: 15-20% volume growth at 75% capacity.
  - Year 3: 15% volume growth at 85% capacity.
