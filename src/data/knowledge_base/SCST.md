# Stand-Up India & National SC-ST Hub Scheme Guidelines (2025)

## 1. Stand-Up India Overview
- **Eligibility**: SC/ST and/or Woman entrepreneurs setting up greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, agri-allied activities, or trading.
- **Loan Size**: Composite loan (inclusive of term loan and working capital) between ₹10 Lakhs and up to ₹1 Crore.
- **Margin Money Requirement**: Standard 15% (can be supplemented with eligible central/state subsidies such that the borrower brings in at least 10% own equity).
- **Repayment Tenor**: Up to 7 years with a maximum moratorium period of 18 months.

## 2. National SC-ST Hub (NSSH) Handholding Support
- 25% mandatory public procurement quota by CPSEs (including 4% from SC/ST and 3% from Women MSMEs).
- Full reimbursement for credit rating, BIS certification, and barcode registration fees.
- Single point registration scheme (SPRS) fee concessions under NSIC.
