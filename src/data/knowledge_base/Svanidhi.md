# PM SVANidhi Operational Guidelines (2025)

## 1. Credit Tranches & Progressive Loan Limits
- **1st Tranche Loan**: Up to ₹10,000 (1-year repayment tenor).
- **2nd Tranche Loan**: Up to ₹20,000 (upon timely or early repayment of 1st tranche, 18-month tenor).
- **3rd Tranche Loan**: Up to ₹50,000 (upon timely repayment of 2nd tranche, up to 36-month tenor).

## 2. Interest Subsidy & Digital Incentives
- **Interest Subsidy**: 7% per annum credited directly into bank account on a quarterly basis for regular repayments.
- **Cashback on Digital Transactions**: Up to ₹1,200 per year (₹100 per month) for accepting UPI / QR code payments.
- **Collateral-Free**: No collateral or guarantor required under CGTMSE credit guarantee cover.
